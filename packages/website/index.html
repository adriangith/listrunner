How it works

The tedious part, automated

Shopping online with a list means pasting each item into the store’s search, one at a time, while keeping track of what’s left. ListRunner runs that loop for you — on the store’s real site, with you still holding the wheel.

  1. 01

    Build your list

    Type it, paste it, or import from Paprika through Apple Reminders. Quantities are understood the way you write them.

    2 apples500g mince1.5L milk3 cans tomatoes
  2. 02

    Shop the real store

    Pick a store and ListRunner opens its actual website — your login, your prices, your delivery options — with the search pre-filled for your current item.

    You choose the exact product. Every time.

  3. 03

    Finish the run

    ListRunner spots your add-to-cart, offers Undo, and advances to the next item. The run ends with a clear handover — checkout stays with you.

    ✓ All items handled. Continue to checkout when ready.

Features

Built for real shopping

Not a cart robot — a shopping companion designed around how people actually buy groceries online.

  • Add-to-cart detection

    On verified stores, ListRunner notices the exact product landing in your cart and checks the item off — with a short Undo window if you change your mind.

  • Lists that parse like you talk

    Whole numbers, decimals, multipliers and units — “2 apples”, “500g mince”, “1.5L milk”. Duplicates merge; input is never rejected.

  • Paprika import

    Export your Paprika grocery list to Apple Reminders and import it in one tap. Items check off back in Reminders as you shop, best-effort.

  • The real store, not a copy

    ListRunner opens the store’s actual website in-app. Your sign-in, your prices, your specials and delivery slots — nothing is proxied or re-sold.

  • Pick up where you left off

    Close the app mid-run and reopen it the same day — ListRunner returns straight to your current item and keeps going.

  • Manual always works

    Automation hiccup? Mark Added, Skip, or search by hand. The list keeps moving — a broken store config never strands you.

  • Accessibility built in

    VoiceOver, Switch Control, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion are supported throughout — the runner announces items, status and position.

  • Private by design

    No account, no analytics, no backend. Your lists and history live on your iPhone and nowhere else.

Safety

You drive. ListRunner navigates.

ListRunner detects what you do — it never shops for you. Four hard lines are built into the app, not just the marketing.

  • ✓

    You pick every product

    ListRunner never chooses, substitutes or reorders. It only runs the sequence of searches and keeps the score.

  • ✓

    You do the checkout

    It never checks out, and never touches payment, delivery or account settings. The run ends before the till.

  • ✓

    Your credentials stay with the store

    Sign-in happens on the store’s own website. ListRunner never sees, stores or sends your password or payment details.

  • ✓

    Your list stays on your phone

    No ListRunner account, no analytics, no backend. Local data you can reset at any time.

Store automation runs only on the store’s own verified web addresses, inside an isolated sandbox the page can’t reach into — and only for the one store you’re shopping.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does ListRunner do my shopping for me?

No. It automates the boring middle — searching each item and tracking progress — on the store’s real website. Every product choice, every substitution and the final checkout stay with you.

How does it know I’ve added an item?

Where automation is verified for your store, ListRunner watches for a confirmed, product-specific add on the store page, then marks the item Added and starts a short Undo window. Where it isn’t, Mark Added and Skip are always one tap away.

What happens if I add the wrong thing?

Undo appears right after a detected add. Where the store supports it, Undo reverses the cart change; otherwise you remove it on the store page and ListRunner follows the cart back down, returning the item to pending.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone. There’s no ListRunner account, no analytics and no backend. Store sign-in happens on the store’s own website, in its own sandboxed website storage.

Can I use my Paprika list?

Yes. Export from Paprika to Apple Reminders, connect once, and ListRunner imports your items with their quantities — checking reminders off again as you shop, best-effort.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on iOS 17 or later. Apple Reminders access is optional and only requested for the Paprika import.

What if my store isn’t listed?

Check the Stores section — new destinations are added regularly. Registered stores without verified automation still work in browse-and-track mode with the full manual flow.
